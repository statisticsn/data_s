所以，關鍵不是「小公司一定不好」，而是 Rivian 能否在擴大規模時，保留品牌特色，同時降低成本和改善服務能力。1

Q16. Rivian 如何體現雙元性發展？

English question

How does Rivian demonstrate ambidextrous development?

中文問題

Rivian 如何體現「雙元性發展」？

Suggested answer｜英文建議答案

Ambidextrous development involves balancing two activities: improving and using existing capabilities, and exploring new opportunities for future growth.

In Rivian’s case, improving existing R1 products and manufacturing processes can be viewed as exploitation. Developing the R2 and exploring new applications for its software capabilities can be viewed as exploration.

The challenge is allocating resources between these activities. If Rivian focuses only on new ideas, it may neglect the efficiency of its current business. But if it focuses only on short-term efficiency, it may miss future opportunities. It needs both to support long-term competitiveness.

中文理解

雙元性發展是指企業同時平衡兩種活動：

Exploitation（深化／利用）：改善和運用現有能力。
Exploration（探索）：尋找未來的新機會。

以 Rivian 為例，改善現有 R1 產品及生產流程，可以視為深化現有能力；開發 R2 和探索軟件的新應用，則可以視為探索新機會。

挑戰在於資源分配：如果只顧探索，可能忽略現有業務效率；但如果只顧短期效率，則可能錯失未來發展機會。因此，長遠競爭力需要兩者兼顧。1

Q17. 假設 R2 需求增加得很快，但 Rivian 無法及時擴產，你會建議怎樣做？

English question

Suppose demand for the R2 grows rapidly, but Rivian cannot increase production fast enough. What would you recommend?

中文問題

假設 R2 的需求迅速增加，但 Rivian 無法及時提高產量，你會提出甚麼建議？

Suggested answer｜英文建議答案

First, I would recommend that Rivian identify the main bottleneck before committing to a large expansion. The constraint might be production capacity, suppliers, workforce, or another part of the process, and each would require a different response.

Second, Rivian should prioritize quality rather than increase output at any cost. Delivering more vehicles with quality problems could create additional service costs and damage customer trust.

Third, the company should communicate realistic delivery expectations to customers and expand service capacity alongside production. This would help Rivian manage growth more responsibly while protecting its brand and customer experience.

中文理解

首先，我會建議 Rivian 先找出最主要的瓶頸，才決定如何擴張。限制可能來自產能、供應商、人手或其他流程，不同原因需要不同解決方法。

其次，公司不應不計代價地追求產量，而應保持品質。如果交付更多有品質問題的車輛，可能增加售後成本，也會損害顧客信任。

最後，Rivian 應向顧客提供合理的交付時間預期，並在擴產時同步提升服務能力。這樣既可以管理增長，也能保護品牌和顧客體驗。

**注意：**這是根據 PPT 提出的「R2 擴產、成本及服務容量」挑戰所作的假設建議，不是簡報聲稱已經發生的事件。1

Q18. 假設 Tesla 大幅減價，Rivian 應否跟隨？

English question

If Tesla significantly reduces the price of a competing SUV, should Rivian follow with a price cut?

中文問題

假設 Tesla 大幅下調競爭 SUV 的價格，Rivian 是否應該跟隨減價？

Suggested answer｜英文建議答案

Not automatically. Rivian should first assess how much the price change affects its target customers and whether matching the discount would damage its financial position.

A price cut might help Rivian remain attractive to some customers, but a smaller company may find it difficult to sustain a prolonged price war. Instead, Rivian could consider a targeted response, such as emphasizing the features and customer experience that distinguish its products.

At the same time, it should continue improving production efficiency so it has more pricing flexibility over time. My recommendation would be to compete on overall customer value, rather than react to every competitor price change.

中文理解

不應該自動跟隨。Rivian 應先評估對手減價對其目標顧客有多大影響，以及跟隨減價會否損害自身財務狀況。

減價可能有助吸引部分顧客，但規模較小的企業未必能長期承受價格戰。Rivian 可以考慮更有針對性的回應，例如強調其產品功能和顧客體驗的獨特價值。

同時，公司應繼續改善生產效率，讓未來有更大的定價彈性。我的建議是以整體顧客價值競爭，而不是每次都跟隨對手的價格變動。這是針對假設情境提出的建議。1
